About Me

Leadership is not about being in charge. It's about taking care of those in your charge.

From Code to Leadership

I started my journey as a curious developer, fascinated by the magic of turning ideas into reality through code. Over 12 years, that curiosity evolved into a passion for building not just software, but high-performing teams.

Today, I lead engineering organizations with a simple belief: great software is built by empowered people. I focus on creating cultures where innovation thrives, mentorship is paramount, and technical excellence is the standard.

When I'm not architecting systems or coaching engineers, you'll find me contributing to open source, writing about leadership, or exploring the intersection of technology and human behavior.
